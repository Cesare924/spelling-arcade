About the app

Spelling Stars is a spelling app for kids in kindergarten through 6th grade. Grown-ups add the school's weekly word list (type it or scan the paper), and kids hear each word, spell it, and earn a token for every word they get right.

✏️ Three ways to practiceHear and spell, unscramble, or fill in missing letters. Letter tiles for little ones, a keyboard for older kids.
📚 The whole school yearKeep every weekly list, practice the whole-year mix, and get ready for test day.
🧠 Smart reviewMissed words come back sooner; mastered words come back now and then so they stick.
🎮 17 gamesBaseball, soccer, field goals, a unicorn dash and more, plus games that use the kid's own spelling words.
👨‍👩‍👧 Made for familiesProfiles for each kid, a grown-up PIN, daily game limits and a progress report.
🔒 Private by designNo accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

Privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Spelling Stars is built for children, so it is designed to collect as little as possible. In short: we don't collect any personal information, and there are no ads, accounts or tracking.

What the app stores

Kid names (or nicknames) and pictures, word lists, practice progress, tokens, scores and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when a grown-up chooses to scan a spelling list. The photo is read on the device to find the words and is not uploaded or kept by the app. On Android, text reading uses Google's on-device ML Kit, which may send Google basic, non-personal information about how the feature performs.

Reading words aloud

Words are spoken by the device's own text-to-speech voices. Depending on your device settings, the voice service from your phone's maker may process the text being spoken.

Backups

If you save a backup, the file goes wherever you choose (for example your email or cloud drive). The app does not upload it anywhere on its own.

Ads, analytics and other companies

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it does not share or sell any information.

Links out of the app

Any link that leaves the app (such as the "Support" link) sits behind a grown-up check, so children can't open it on their own.

Children's privacy

Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing for us to access, change or delete. Parents can remove all data at any time by deleting the app or using the reset options in the app's settings.
